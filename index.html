Principles

The beliefs that give the venture portfolio a clear and differentiated point of view.

  1. I

    Build capacity, not fixes

    Prioritise the knowledge, tools and systems that give people greater agency at the next challenge.

  2. II

    Regenerate before expanding

    Treat sustainable land, crops and infrastructure as the foundation of resilient human progress.

  3. III

    Think in decades, not quarters

    Compound the portfolio through patient action, stewardship and durable institutions.

  4. IV

    Prepare Earth for space

    Begin with the conditions that sustain life here, then extend that capability beyond Earth.

Meet David

David Swift is an entrepreneur turned farmer and technologist — a long-horizon founder building carbon-negative infrastructure and human capability. Founder of MacroAgri in Abu Dhabi and partner in Plant Force Britain, he brings permaculture, the decades-ahead whole-systems design methodology rooted in farming, to modern technology and infrastructure.
